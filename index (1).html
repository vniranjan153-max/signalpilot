How to read this

  • P(up): the model's estimated chance the price is higher in 5 trading days. 50% means no opinion.
  • BUY / SELL / HOLD: BUY when P(up) is above the buy threshold, SELL (exit / avoid) when below the sell threshold, otherwise HOLD.
  • Model quality: out-of-sample AUC from walk-forward testing. Near 0.50 = coin flip; ignore the signal. Real edges in daily markets are small and unstable.
  • Backtest: long-only, trained only on past data at each step, after assumed trading costs. It is still a simulation.
